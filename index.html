About

Hi, I'm Saba, a Data Science master's student at TU Wien, supported by the Helmut Veith Scholarship. I hold a bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Shahid Beheshti University, where I first developed my interests in artificial intelligence through research at the intersection of AI and the brain.

My current research interests lie in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, with a focus on Natural Language Processing and Generative AI. I am particularly interested in large language models, agentic AI, and trustworthy AI, with the goal of developing intelligent, reliable, and scalable AI systems.

During my bachelor’s studies, I gained research experience across computational neuroscience, neural signal analysis, federated learning, and scientific machine learning. These experiences form the foundation of my current direction in AI research and engineering.

Sogand Rabipour, Saba Madadi, Hossein Azari · International Congress on Fuzzy & Intelligent Systems of Iran

Bachelor's thesis · 2026 · Grade: 20/20

DLT-SNN: Dynamic Learnable Spatio-Temporal Threshold Adaptation in Spiking Neural Networks

Bachelor's thesis at Shahid Beheshti University, supervised by Dr. S. R. Kheradpisheh. Investigated a context-conditioned adaptive firing-threshold mechanism (ThetaLIF) for spiking neural networks. The study compared learned dynamic thresholds with fixed-threshold LIF neurons on MNIST, examining class-selective excitability, classification accuracy, and spike activity. Awarded the full grade of 20/20.
